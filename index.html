CH 01

About

Most of the work on this website entails concepts in RF/microwave engineering and machine learning — designing and benchmarking transmitter chains while operating RF measurement rigs and connectorized components.

My day-to-day spans system modeling, test automation, and telecom subsystem analysis, with a focus on spectral efficiency, signal integrity, and repeatable lab workflows.

Current research centers on power-amplifier linearization using advanced ML, including architecture-matched modeling to align nonlinearity behavior across similar PA topologies. I received an AUS Undergraduate Research Grant for this effort and continue to refine compensation strategies that improve EVM and adjacent-channel performance under realistic drive conditions — in addition to another grant for my work on an ADRV9026-based measurement system.

More personal work includes…

  • Designing a Phase-Locked Loop for a Frequency-Modulated Continuous-Wave Ground Penetrating Radar using ADIsimPLL and Analog Devices’ ADF4159, achieving exceptionally low frequency and phase error for clean chirp generation and robust time–frequency coherence. System build ongoing
  • Earning a professional certificate in PCB fabrication (2023), building a 1.8 GHz Class-A single-transistor PA, and executing multi-layer PCB processes from layout through chemical etching.
  • Planning complete RF line-ups in Keysight ADS to a fixed budget and target transmission characteristics (C/IMD3 and P1dB), translating datasheet constraints into practical design trade-offs.
  • Outside the lab, organizing an IEEE ComSoc workshop at AUS using SDRs and GNU Radio — demonstrating analog modulation chains and digital payload construction with BPSK, QPSK, and QAM.

CH 04

Research — ML-driven DPD

Advanced Radio Technologies Lab

Measurement-driven ML-DPD: align measured PA input/output IQ, transform the samples into memory-aware features, train behavioral PA models, and use those models to evaluate or train neural DPD systems.

Most experiments used measured IQ data from the team's ADRV9026-MB evaluation-board system. Over time the work expanded from BiLSTM behavioral modeling into spectral-loss DPD, cross-PA transfer, efficient input selection, pruning, and hybrid least-squares/neural regression.

PA1 / PA2 behavioral models

  • Built BiLSTM behavioral PA models that capture memory effects and support differentiable cascaded DPD training.
  • Implemented a spectral-output-aware DPD loss that directly penalizes adjacent-channel leakage.
  • Investigated cross-PA transfer by freezing learned recurrent PA dynamics and retraining lightweight adaptation layers.
  • Developed memory-polynomial input matrix selection methods for efficient PA modeling.
  • Explored neural model pruning for RVTDNN/ARVTDNN deployment efficiency.
  • Tested hybrid least-squares/neural regression layers as a bridge between GMP-style modeling and neural feature learning.
